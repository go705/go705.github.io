Quantum noise fundamentally limits measurement sensitivity, but squeezed states of light provide a route to surpass the standard quantum limit. In this work, we demonstrate the first fully resonant quasi-phase-matched χ(2) optical parametric amplifier on a photonic chip using a periodically poled thin-film lithium niobate microresonator. By simultaneously achieving near-full-depth domain inversion, intrinsic quality factors exceeding 2.5 million, and escape efficiencies above 90%, we generate broadband continuous-wave squeezed light around 1587 nm with only 27 mW pump power. We directly observe −0.81 dB of squeezing and infer an on-chip squeezing level of −7.52 dB, representing the highest squeezing ratio reported for an integrated χ(2) cavity platform. The demonstrated 10.3 THz squeezing bandwidth establishes a scalable route toward compact, power-efficient sources for quantum sensing, metrology, communications, and continuous-variable quantum information processing.
