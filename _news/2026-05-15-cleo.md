Bo-Han Wu participated in the Conference on Lasers and Electro-Optics (CLEO) 2026 in Charlotte, North Carolina.

During the conference, he delivered an invited talk in the research of Bidirectional Nonlinear Optical Tomography. He also served as session chair for the technical session on **Novel Quantum Processing**.
