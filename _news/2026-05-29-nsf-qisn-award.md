---
title: "NSF Awards $600K Grant for Quantum Intelligent Sensor Networks"
date: 2026-05-29
---

We are excited to announce that the National Science Foundation (NSF) has awarded a $600,000 grant to support a new research project on **Quantum Intelligent Sensor Networks (QISN)** at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa.

The project combines quantum sensing, integrated photonics, and artificial intelligence to develop adaptive sensor networks capable of detecting weak signals beyond classical limits. The research is led by **Bo-Han Wu** at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa in collaboration with **[Hyeongrak ("Chuck") Choi](https://qep.ece.stonybrook.edu/team/)** at Stony Brook University.
